## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This document establishes the line-by-line evidentiary audit of every claim presented in `16_Everyday_Life_and_Personal_Details.json`. 

In previous versions, details from secondary visual adaptations (such as Ufotable film background assets, merchandise, or drama CDs) and reasonable roleplay inferences were occasionally aggregated under a broad `[CANON]` banner. This audit rigorously separates:

1. **`[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`**: Explicitly established in Kinoko Nasu's original *Kara no Kyoukai* light novels (1998–2004), *Mirai Fukuin*, or foundational Type-Moon reference materials (*Kara no Kyoukai Material*, *Type-Moon Character Material*).
2. **`[CANON - ADAPTATION / VISUAL MEDIA]`**: Introduced or visually codified in the Ufotable theatrical film adaptations (2007–2013) or official Type-Moon visual artwork, but absent or unstated in the original prose.
3. **`[INFERENCE]`**: Logical deductions grounded directly in established character psychology, historical circumstance, or physiological state.
4. **`[RP SUGGESTION]`**: Optional, non-conflicting flavor details designed to enrich natural roleplay dialogue and domestic scenes.
5. **`[CANON - UNKNOWN]`**: Explicitly acknowledged gaps where the source material provides no canonical preference, preventing artificial fabrication.
6. **`[CANON - CONFIRMED NONE]`**: Canonical confirmation that a character intentionally lacks or physically cannot have secular dining, food preferences, or domestic routines.

## 2. Character-by-Character Canon Audit & Corrections

### 1. Ryougi Shiki (両儀式)

#### A. Food & Drink Habits
* **Strawberry Ice Cream (`イチゴのアイス`)**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & FILM]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 3 (*Remaining Sense of Pain*) and Chapter 5 (*Paradox Spiral*). Mikiya specifically purchases strawberry ice cream for Shiki and brings it to her apartment. Shiki claims she isn't interested but pulls the tub closer and eats it before her meals.
* **Häagen-Dazs Branded Container**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - FILM ADAPTATION]`
  - *Source*: In the Ufotable theatrical adaptation of Chapters 3 and 5, the ice cream container is drawn with explicit Häagen-Dazs branding (official anime product placement/detail). In the novel prose, it is simply referred to as strawberry ice cream.
* **Strawberry Pocky / Biscuit Sticks**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - ADAPTATION / OFFICIAL ART]`
  - *Source*: Prominently featured in official Type-Moon visual illustrations, anime promo art, and the *Melty Blood* crossover appearances. In the original novel text, Nasu does not mention "Pocky" by trademark name; it is an iconic visual shorthand.
* **Canned Hot Black Coffee**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & FILM]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 7 (*Murder Speculation Part 2*). While waiting on cold nocturnal streets near Mifune Station, Shiki buys hot canned coffee from a street vending machine and thrusts it against Mikiya's chest to warm him.
* **Tea Preferences**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & FILM]`
  - *Source*: Canned green tea and hot roasted barley tea (mugicha) brewed by Mikiya in her apartment.
* **Culinary Skill**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 3 and 5. Her kitchen is practically bare. She has an electric kettle and chopsticks, but possesses zero domestic cooking skills, subsisting on convenience store rolls (*anpan*) and instant noodles when Mikiya does not cook for her.
* **Alcohol Consumption**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - UNKNOWN]`
  - *Source*: Shiki does not casually drink alcohol in canon. In *Mirai Fukuin*, when meeting Mikiya as adults, drinking is not portrayed as a personal hobby. Marked as strictly unknown rather than invented.

#### B. Wardrobe & Personal Style
* **Iconic Outdoor Ensemble (Kimono + Red Leather Jacket + Combat Boots)**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 1 onwards. Light-blue (*mizuiro*) or white unpatterned kimono worn beneath a single-rider red leather motorcycle jacket, paired with dark brown lace-up combat boots.
* **Indoor Lounging vs. Outdoor Outerwear**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 1 (*Overlooking View*). Indoors in Room 404, Shiki lounges solely in her light-blue kimono (*mizuiro no kimono* / *hitoe*) or white under-robe (*nagajuban* / *nemaki*). The red leather jacket is strictly outdoor outerwear (*uwagi*); the text explicitly notes: *"When I left the house, I put on a red leather jacket over my light blue kimono"* (家を出るとき、水色の着物の上に赤い革ジャンを羽織った). She does NOT sit around indoors wearing the leather jacket unless heading out or if the room is freezing in the morning.
* **Refusal of Western Clothes (The Retainer Backstory)**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - OFFICIAL MATERIAL]`
  - *Source*: *Kara no Kyoukai Complete Material*. Raised in the Ryougi estate wearing *wafuku* exclusively. When enrolling in high school, her caretaker and family retainer Suzurigi Akitaka (硯木秋隆) purchased modern Western adolescent clothes for her; Shiki flatly refused to wear them, insisting on traditional kimonos. Her kimonos are bespoke haute-couture garments supplied by the clan and laundered/repaired by Akitaka after battles.
* **Undergarments (Classical Kimono Custom)**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - OFFICIAL CREATOR Q&A]`
  - *Source*: Official *Kara no Kyoukai Movie 2: A Study in Murder (Part 1)* theater pamphlet Q&A. When asked if Shiki goes braless under her kimono, Takashi Takeuchi and Kinoko Nasu confirmed that Shiki follows classical Japanese kimono tradition: she does not wear Western underwear (braless, no panties) under her daily kimono and robes.
* **Sleepwear & Morning Wear (*Nemaki* & Low Morning Energy)**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapters 1 and 5. Sleeps in a plain white cotton *nemaki* (寝間着, sleeping yukata) or *nagajuban* (長襦袢) with a loose cloth sash, or collapses in her daytime kimono when exhausted. Experiences severe low morning blood pressure (*teiketsuatsu*) and bedhead (*neguse*), moving groggily upon waking. When Mikiya visits in the morning, she has zero feminine modesty or embarrassment, remaining curled or cross-legged on her futon in her disheveled morning robe and greeting him with sleepy grumbles (*"Noisy, Kokutou... what time is it?"*).
* **Haircut & Grooming**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 4 (*Hollow Shrine*). Long hair prior to 1996; in June 1998, upon waking from her coma, she cuts her hair roughly with a knife in the hospital bathroom mirror. Never uses pins, ribbons, makeup, or perfume.
* **Pockets & Gestures**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Habitually buries both hands deep inside her leather jacket pockets; pulls her collar up over her mouth and chin when flustered or embarrassed by Mikiya.

### 4. Asagami Fujino (浅上藤乃)

#### A. Sensory Capabilities: Congenital vs. Chemically Induced Analgesia
* **CRITICAL AUDIT & CORRECTION**:
  - *Previous Classification Error*: Previously described as having "congenital analgesia".
  - *Verified Canon Fact*: **Her pain insensitivity was NOT congenital.**
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 3 (*Remaining Sense of Pain*). Fujino was born with innate telekinetic psychic channels (the ancestral power of the Asagami clan). During her childhood, her father discovered her abilities and, terrified of societal ostracization, systematically administered nerve-deadening drugs / spinal anesthesia to numb her sensory nervous system. The lack of pain, temperature, and taste was an **artificially induced pharmaceutical seal** to keep her psychic power dormant.
* **Awakening via Appendicitis**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 3. During the assault by delinquents, her inflamed, ruptured appendix generated internal pressure and agony so intense that it shattered the chemical block, violently restoring her sense of pain and unlocking her Mystic Eyes of Distortion.
* **Post-Bridge Sensory Reality**:
  - *Evidentiary Status*: `[CANON - NOVEL & PRIMARY]`
  - *Source*: Novel Chapter 3. Shiki severed her appendicitis rather than her life. Her restored nervous system remains functional, leaving her physically vulnerable, delicate, and sensitive to cold.
